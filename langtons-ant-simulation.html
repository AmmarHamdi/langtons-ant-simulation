✦ The surprising part

With one ant, around 10,000 steps in, the chaos gives way to a repeated diagonal “highway”: a cycle of 104 steps.

When is there a collision? At the end of each simulation step, 2 or more ants on the same cell make one collision event for that cell. Three ants together count as three pair contacts. Events are counted again if ants overlap in later steps. A glowing orange marker highlights any currently occupied collision cell. Passing one another between cells is not counted.

Just two rules. That's all.

How can order emerge?

One ant begins on an infinitely large, all-white grid. The rules are deterministic. After roughly 10,000 steps the ant forms a repeating diagonal trail.

The single-ant highway has been observed to continue in simulations, but its continuation forever has not been proved.
